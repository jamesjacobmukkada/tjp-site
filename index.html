Our Manifesto

Eight promises. Ethinum Leader-inte pocket-ilninnu oru roopa polum pokilla.

1. The Right to Lunch

Food is a fundamental right. Anyone who says "just wait 10 more minutes" will be put on trial in a people’s court. Never say it again, you scoundrels!

2. The Vellamadi Rule

No vellamadi party with the Leader shall end before 9am the next day.

3. Membership Yogyatha

Every member of the Party must have watched Salaar or Barroz at least twice. Those who haven’t will have their membership cancelled, no questions asked.

4. Vandi Virudha Nayam

The Party stands firmly against driving. All comrades must use the metro or public transport. (The real reason: petrol costs money.)

5. Arsenal Before Everything

The Party will win the league by 2030. If it doesn't, the referee, VAR and the Tories will be held responsible.

6. Scientific Bill Splitting

Every comrade pays exactly for what they ate. No more "just split it equally" capitalism.

7. The Right to Roast

Every stupid, boring, two-and-a-half-hour movie shall be sat through by the Leader on YouTube, in a 20 minute roast, so the people don’t have to suffer.

8. Second Show Rights

Watching the same film twice in a theatre is not a waste of money, it is research. The second ticket shall be tax-free (and paid by someone else).
